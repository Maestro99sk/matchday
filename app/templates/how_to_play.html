How to Play

World Cup 5s · WC 2026

The basics

Each matchday you pick a team of 5 starters from the players appearing in that day's World Cup fixtures. Stay under your budget, pick your formation, and score points based on what your players do on the pitch.

Budget

Every player starts with a 500 budget. You can earn extra budget by referring friends — each referral adds 2%, up to a maximum of +20%.

Player prices are adjusted each matchday based on fixture difficulty: players facing a tough opponent are cheaper, players facing a weaker side cost more.

Nation rules

  • Maximum 2 starters from the same nation (3 on single-game days).
  • Each sub must be from the same nation as the starter they cover.

Subs bench

Alongside your 5 starters you pick 4 subs — one per position in your formation. If a starter doesn't play (not in the starting XI or substituted off before kick-off), their sub automatically replaces them — as long as the sub is from the same nation.

Scoring

EventPoints
Goal scored+10

Clean sheet (GK / DEF)+6
Clean sheet (MID)+3

Red card−5

A clean sheet means your player's team conceded zero goals in the match. Forwards do not earn clean sheet points.

Lock time

Lineups lock before the first kick-off of each matchday. You cannot change your picks after the lock. Check the Schedule for exact lock times.

Leagues

Create or join a private league with friends. Each league has its own leaderboard. You can be in multiple leagues at once — you pick a separate team for each league per matchday.
